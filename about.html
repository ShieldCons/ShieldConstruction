About Shield Construction LLC

Georgia licensed restoration and reconstruction professionals — locally owned, customer-first, available 24/7.

Our Story

At Shield Construction, we believe restoring a property is about more than repairing damage—it is about helping families and business owners recover from unexpected events and move forward with confidence.

Founded in Georgia, Shield Construction was built on a commitment to providing professional water damage restoration, mitigation, reconstruction, and property repair services with honesty, quality, and attention to detail. We understand that water damage, storm damage, and property emergencies can be overwhelming, which is why our team focuses on responding quickly, communicating clearly, and delivering solutions that restore both the property and peace of mind.

Our experience spans residential and commercial projects, from emergency water extraction and structural drying to complete reconstruction and remodeling. By combining industry-standard restoration practices with skilled construction expertise, we are able to manage projects from initial mitigation through final reconstruction, providing our clients with a seamless restoration experience.

We work closely with homeowners, property managers, insurance carriers, and adjusters to ensure every project is documented, communicated, and completed professionally. Our goal is to restore every property to its pre-loss condition while maintaining the highest standards of workmanship and customer service.

At Shield Construction, we don’t simply repair buildings—we help protect what matters most.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional emergency restoration and reconstruction services that protect property, support insurance claims, and exceed customer expectations across Metro Atlanta.
