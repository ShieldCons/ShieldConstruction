About Shield Construction LLC

Georgia licensed restoration and reconstruction professionals — locally owned, customer-first, available 24/7.

Our Story

Shield Construction LLC was founded with a simple mission: provide Metro Atlanta homeowners and businesses with fast, honest, and professional restoration services when they need them most.

From emergency water extraction in Duluth to full reconstruction in Alpharetta, our team combines licensed contracting expertise with compassionate customer care. We understand that property damage is stressful — that's why we guide you through every step, including insurance coordination.

As a locally owned company, we're invested in our communities across Gwinnett, Fulton, Forsyth, and Hall counties. When you call Shield Construction, you're not just getting a contractor — you're getting a partner dedicated to restoring your property and peace of mind.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional emergency restoration and reconstruction services that protect property, support insurance claims, and exceed customer expectations across Metro Atlanta.
